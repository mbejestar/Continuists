About

Work should not disappear when its creator steps away.

Continuum is designed around structured mission records, controlled access, collaboration and optional transfer.

How the model works

Creators document a problem, findings, lessons, funding and value. Missions are private by default. A creator may later make a mission public. Public missions can receive collaboration requests; private missions require direct invitations.

Marketplace

When a mission is listed for sale, the marketplace displays the heading and problem statement while protected content remains inaccessible until the buyer has completed the purchase and receives the agreed legal access.
