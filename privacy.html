Privacy

Privacy Notice

Draft privacy notice for the prototype. Final wording and operational processes should be reviewed by a South African privacy professional.

Information collected

Account details, contact information, mission content, access records, collaboration requests, messages, transaction records, beneficiary details and technical information may be processed where necessary for the service.

Purposes

Information may be used to provide the service, authenticate users, preserve provenance, facilitate collaboration, process transactions, manage subscriptions, detect abuse, meet legal obligations and protect the rights of users and the platform.

POPIA principles

The production service should implement appropriate safeguards, purpose limitation, data minimisation, transparency, retention controls, data subject rights and security measures consistent with POPIA and applicable regulations.

Sharing

Protected mission content should not be disclosed to marketplace viewers before the applicable access conditions are satisfied. Personal information should only be shared where there is a lawful basis and the disclosure is necessary for the stated purpose.

Security and retention

The production system should use access controls, encryption where appropriate, audit logs, backups, breach procedures and defined retention/deletion schedules. The prototype stores data locally in a browser and is not suitable for production personal-data processing.

Requests and complaints

Provide a documented channel for access, correction, deletion where legally available, objection, complaints and other data-subject requests. The production service should identify its Information Officer and publish the appropriate contact details.
